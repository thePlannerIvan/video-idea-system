# Commercial Use

Video Idea System is open source under AGPL-3.0. The community edition is designed to be useful, inspectable, and modifiable.

Commercial support and licensing are available separately for teams that need private deployment, closed-source integration, or a customized ideation pipeline.

## Available Commercial Work

- private workflow deployment
- enterprise method-library adaptation (house divergence methods, critique checklists, Method Wiki seeding)
- brand and channel content-system design
- custom validators and content-contract quality gates
- private review dashboards
- internal training for agent-assisted ideation and spoken-script workflows
- closed-source commercial licensing

## When To Contact

Contact the maintainer if you want to:

- integrate Video Idea System into a closed-source product;
- offer a hosted service based on a modified version;
- build a private company version with non-public methods, templates, or scripts;
- use the project name, author identity, or brand in commercial promotion;
- commission a custom ideation-to-script workflow for a team or client.
